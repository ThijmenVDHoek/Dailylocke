DAILYLOCKE

A daily nuzlocke challenge. Five sections, one run per day.

Free Play

How it works

1
Two ways to play

Daily is the same fixed 5-section challenge for everyone, scored and recorded once a day. Free Play is endless and randomized. They use separate save slots, so one never blocks the other.

2
Pick a starter

Choose 1 of 3, name it, and set out.

3
A section is 4 battles

Three wild encounters, then a trainer with a full team and smarter tactics.

4
Nothing resets

HP, PP and status carry across every battle. Beating the trainer fully heals your survivors — nothing else does.

5
Fainted is forever

A Pokemon that falls is gone for good and listed in the Memorial. There are no revives.

6
You can always run

Fleeing a wild battle never fails, but you forfeit that battle's prize money. Trainers will not let you walk away.

7
One catch per section

Only the first wild encounter can be caught, and duplicates are re-rolled. Max 6 in the party.

★
Shinies break the rules

A 1 in 512 chance on any wild or starter. Shinies are always catchable, never break free, and are kept forever in your Shiny Collection.

8
Spend your winnings

Shop the Mart for balls, medicine, held items, evolution stones and Mega Stones. Train any Pokemon to change its moves, ability, nature and Stat Points.

9
The run ends when your last Pokemon falls

Your score is battles won, and your top damage dealer is crowned MVP.

▲
Ascension

Past Section 15 in Free Play, difficulty stops being about bigger numbers. Each tier adds battle-start weather, terrain or hazards, elite Pokemon with a visible modifier, sharper AI, boss clauses every 5 sections, and reduced section healing. Everything is previewed on the route screen before you commit, and rewards flatten out as it climbs.
